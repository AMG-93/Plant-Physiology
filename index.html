THE CELL CYCLE (Overview) ￼ G₀Quiescent / non-dividing state G₁Cell growth and normal functions SDNA replication G₂Preparation for mitosis MMitosis and cytokinesis MITOSIS STAGES (What to look for) INTERPHASE Intact nucleus, diffuse chromatin, nucleolus visible. PROPHASE Chromatin condenses into visible chromosomes. METAPHASE Chromosomes align at the equatorial plate. ANAPHASE Sister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles. TELOPHASE Chromosomes at poles; nuclei reform; cell plate begins. FROM CELL DIVISION TO PLANT TISSUES The onion root-tip cells in this lab divide rapidly in the apical meristem. After mitosis, daughter cells grow and differentiate into specialized plant tissues. In a mature leaf, these include dermal tissue (including stomata for gas exchange), ground tissue, and vascular tissue, which transports water, minerals, and sugars. 1. Why would an onion root tip have a higher mitotic index than a mature leaf? 2. Which tissue system transports water and sugars through the plant? 6.
